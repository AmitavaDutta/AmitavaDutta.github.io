I am a PhD student in Geodynamics at the Department of Earth and Climate Sciences (ECS), IISER Pune, supported by a CSIR-MoES JRF fellowship. My interests lie in Solid Earth Geophysics and Geodynamics, with a particular focus on understanding crustal evolution, lithospheric deformation, and plate-boundary dynamics. My research brings together geodynamic modelling, seismology, thermodynamics, and empirical approaches to investigate the physical state and evolution of the Earth's interior. I am particularly interested in using observations such as temperature estimates, seismic velocity anomalies, and density variations to better understand the processes that govern the deformation and evolution of the lithosphere. Ultimately, my work aims to bridge the gap between temperature, composition, and mineral physics, and to use these connections to better understand the geodynamic processes shaping our planet. Before starting my PhD, I completed a 5-year Integrated BSMS Dual Degree Programme with a major in Geological Sciences at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Kolkata. I then worked as a Research Assistant at ECS, IISER Pune, from August 2024 to July 2025. Finally, this website is where I try to put together the things I work on, the things I learn, and occasionally, the things that somehow worked after staring at the code for far too long.
